# On your phone

Ferret is a web app you can install, so it opens full screen like any other app.

Open Ferret on a phone or tablet and it offers to do this for you (**Put Ferret on your Home Screen**, on the sign-in
screen and the home screen). **Not now** hides the offer on that device for a month.

## iPhone and iPad

![The sign-in screen on an iPhone offering to put Ferret on the Home Screen](/screenshots/install-light.webp){.shot .light-only}
![The sign-in screen on an iPhone offering to put Ferret on the Home Screen](/screenshots/install-dark.webp){.shot .dark-only}

Open Ferret in **Safari** (Chrome and Firefox work too). The offer shows the steps for your browser; **Show me where**
points an arrow at the first button. In Safari:

- **iPhone, iOS 26**: tap **⋯** at the bottom right, beside the address → **Share** → scroll down → **Add to Home Screen**
  → **Add**.
- **iPhone, earlier iOS**: tap **Share** (the square with an arrow) in the bar at the bottom → **Add to Home Screen** →
  **Add**.
- **iPad**: tap **Share** at the top right, beside the address → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

No bar at the bottom? Tap the very bottom of the screen and it comes back. No **Add to Home Screen** in the list?
Scroll to the bottom of it, tap **Edit Actions**, and add it. In **Chrome**, **Share** is in the address bar at the top
right; in **Firefox**, it's in the **☰** menu.

Then open Ferret from its new icon and sign in once; it stays signed in.

The installed app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari's — so add it first, then sign in there.

## Android

Tap **Install** on the offer, or open Ferret in Chrome and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**) from the ⋮
menu.

## What works best on a phone

- The **camera**: add things from photos, scan barcodes and QR labels, photograph receipts as documents.
- **Push reminders**: turn them on for each device in **Household & settings → Notifications**. On an iPhone they
  need Ferret installed on the Home Screen first.
- The tab bar along the bottom: Home, Everything, Places, Maintenance and Settings.

## No signal

In a basement, a storage unit or a barn with no signal, Ferret still opens (once it's been used on that phone while
online) and says **Offline**. You can keep going:

- **Take a photo** or **From photos** — each becomes a **New item** to name later, in the room you pick (optional).
- **Scan** — choose what a scan means first: **Used one**, **Restock**, **Add** or **Find**.

Everything waits on the phone and uploads by itself when there's signal again (or tap **Try again**). A scan sent twice
still counts once. If a scan needs an answer — the code is on several things, the thing is set to ask, you already have it (add to it, or add a separate one), or nothing has
that code yet — it waits on the home screen under **Captured offline** for you to choose.

What's kept on the phone for this: the household's name and its rooms, and the photos and scans until they upload.
Signing out clears it all. Nothing else of your inventory is stored on the phone.

## Light or dark

**Appearance** in the menu: **Auto** follows your phone's setting (the default), or choose **Light** or
**Dark**. It's per device, and this help follows the same choice. Printed reports and labels are always light.
