# Repairs and cost of ownership

## Logging a repair

When something gets fixed, open its page and tap **Repairs → Log a repair**:

- **What was done** (*Replaced the drain pump*) and **what went wrong** (*Wouldn't drain, error E21*),
- **when**, **what it cost**, and **who did it**: one of your [service providers](./maintenance#service-providers),
  or *We did*,
- the **parts** used (part numbers help next time),
- **photos or the invoice**: before and after, the bill. They're kept with the thing's other photos and documents
  and shown with the repair,
- if the repair comes with its own guarantee, **when that ends**. It's added to the thing's
  [warranties](./warranties), so you'll hear before it runs out,
- if it was part of a [maintenance task](./maintenance), which one.

Repairs show in the thing's history (*Repaired Washer: Replaced the drain pump ($180.00)*). **Edit** changes one, or
removes it. Its photos and warranty stay with the thing.

Maintenance is for work that repeats (a filter every three months). A repair is a one-off.

## Cost of owning it

Once a thing has had maintenance or repairs with a cost, its page adds them up under **Cost of owning it**:

- what you paid for it,
- plus what its done [maintenance](./maintenance) cost,
- plus its repairs,
- minus what it sold for, or what insurance paid out, once it's gone.

After a couple of years it also shows the cost per year owned, and when it's
[likely due for replacement](./replacements). Together they help with *repair or replace?*

## Replaced by

When something is replaced, mark the old one gone (**Edit → No longer have it?**) and pick the new one under
**Replaced by**. Each page then links to the other: *Replaced by New fridge* on the old one, *Replaces Old fridge*
on the new one.

[Claude](./claude) can log repairs too (*"the plumber replaced the water heater's thermostat today, $180"*), and
tell you what something has cost so far.
