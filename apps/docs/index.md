## Talk to us

Questions, ideas, or something odd in the demo? Join the [Ferret Discord](https://discord.gg/XSkKyP2pdg) — there's a
forum for help, another for feature ideas, and you'll hear about new releases there first.

Ferret is open source (MIT): the code, bug reports and releases are on [GitHub](https://github.com/ferret-project/ferret).
