# Warranties and claims

## Warranties

On an item's page, add a warranty: when it ends (and starts), who provides it, what it covers, and the document it's
on. Ferret can suggest one when it reads a warranty card or a receipt — tap it to add it.

The home screen shows warranties ending soon, and the bell lists those ending within a month (see
[Needs attention](./attention)). Within a week, the daily reminder mentions them too.

## Claims

When something breaks under warranty, file a **claim** under that warranty: what went wrong, the provider's claim or
case number, and how it went — **filed → in progress → approved or denied → resolved**, with what it cost you (a
deductible, shipping). A claim with no news for two weeks shows up in the bell as a nudge to follow up.
