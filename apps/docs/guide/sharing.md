# Share links

A **share link** shows part of your inventory to someone without a Ferret account — an insurance adjuster after a
claim, an executor, a family member — read-only, for a while.

On a hosted Ferret, share links may come with some plans only — see [Plan and usage](./plan-and-usage).

## Making one

**Share…** is on the [insurance report](./insurance-report) (the whole household, or the property you picked), and on
a category's or tag's page (*everything tagged Jewelry*). Only the household's owner and admins see it.

- **Who it's for** — just a note for you (*State Farm claim*), shown on their page too.
- **Works for** 7, 30 or 90 days.
- **Photos, receipts and manuals too** — on by default: each item's files open from the link, which is the proof an
  insurer asks for. Off: just the list.

Then **Copy link** (or **Send…** on a phone) and send it however you like.

## What they see

The same report you'd print: totals, properties, what's in each room, every item with its make, model, serial
number, price and value — and, with files, its photos and receipts. They can print it, save it as a PDF, or download
it as a spreadsheet. It's worked out when they open it, so it's always up to date. **Your notes are never shown.**

## Keeping track

**Settings → Household → Share links** lists every link: still open, turned off, or run out, and how many times each
was opened — tap that to see when, and from which address. **Turn off…** stops a link straight away.

::: tip Reaching it
A link opens only where Ferret can be reached. If your Ferret is on your home network only, whoever opens it needs to
be on that network (or your VPN) — see [Away from home](../hosting/away-from-home).
:::
