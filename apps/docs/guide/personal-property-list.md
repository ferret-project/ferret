# Personal property list

A printable list of who should have what one day — your things grouped by person, each with a photo and what tells
it apart (brand, model, serial number, where it's kept), and a line to sign and date under each person. Keep it with
your other papers, or give your family a copy.

## Naming who gets something

On a thing's page, **Edit → Goes to (heir)** and type a name. Write the same name the same way each time (upper and
lower case and extra spaces don't matter: "Sam" and " sam " are one person).

## The list

**Personal property list** (in the menu, under Insurance) lists everyone you've named, alphabetically, with their
things. Turn **Photos** and **Values** on or off, then **Print or save as PDF** — each person starts on a new page.
Things you no longer have (see [Past things](./past-things)) are left out.

Only the household's **owner and admins** can open it, since it says who gets what. Others in the household still see
the "Goes to" field on a thing's page.

::: warning Not a will
This is a list of wishes kept in Ferret, not a legal document. Whether a list like this counts — and what makes it
count (a will that refers to it, signatures, witnesses, dates) — depends on where you live. Ask a lawyer.
:::
