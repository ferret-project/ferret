# Room review and last seen

An inventory is only useful if it's still right. Things get moved, lent, thrown out or lost, and the list
slowly drifts away from what's really in the house. Ferret keeps track of when each thing was last seen, and a room
review lets you check a whole room in a few minutes.

## Last seen

A thing counts as seen when someone:

- scans its [QR label](./labels), barcode or pairing code (with the phone's camera or Ferret's scanner),
- taps **Still here** on its page, or
- checks it in a room review.

Its page says when, and who, under *Added…*: *Seen 3 days ago by Sam*, *Checked in a room review 2 months ago*.
A box's **Still here** counts for everything in it too. A box's contents list says when each thing was last seen.

**Everything → Last seen** finds what nobody has seen in a month, six months or a year. A thing added more recently
doesn't count, because adding it is seeing it. Sorting by **Not seen the longest** puts the oldest first.

A scan only means someone looked the code up, maybe while searching. **Still here** and room reviews mean
someone actually looked.

## Reviewing a room

**Room review** (in the menu under Lists, or **Review this room** on a room's page) lists your places, starting with
the one checked longest ago: places never reviewed first, the fullest of those first. Open one and tap
**Start the review**, then go through what's there:

- **Still here**: it's where Ferret says it is.
- **Moved…**: pick where it is now. It's moved, and counted as seen there.
- **Gone…**: sold, given away, thrown out, lost or stolen. The same choices as
  [No longer have it?](./past-things#marking-something-gone).
- **Can't find it**: it goes on the [Can't find](#can-t-find-it) list.

Only the things kept right in that place are listed. A box counts for everything inside it, and each place inside
(a shelf, a cupboard) gets a review of its own, linked at the bottom. **3 of 12 checked** shows how far you are. You
can stop and carry on later, and so can anyone else in your household. Tapped the wrong thing? **Change** it.

When everything's checked, **Finish the review**. The place now shows when it was last reviewed, and *Recent
changes* on the dashboard says what you found (*Reviewed Garage: 38 still here, 1 moved, 1 can't find*).
**Finish now** works part way through too, leaving the rest unchecked.

## Can't find it

**Can't find it** on a thing's page or in a review marks it missing. It still counts as yours (in totals and the
insurance report) until either:

- **Found it** (or **Still here**, or checking it in a room review): it's back, and its history says *Found …*, or
- **It's lost**: it's [marked gone](./past-things) as lost, ready for a claim if it comes to that.

**Can't find** in the menu lists everything missing, with both buttons on each.

Scanned it? Opening a missing thing by its label or barcode shows the same banner on its page, and the home scanner
(Use up or Restock) asks *Found it?* under the count.

## On the dashboard and in the bell

The dashboard's **Keeping track** card shows how many things nobody can find, how many haven't been seen in a year,
and the place to review next. The [bell](./attention) mentions both counts too, gently. They're never urgent, so
they're not in the daily reminder.

Viewers see when things were last seen but can't mark them or review rooms.

## With Claude

[Claude](./claude) can do the same: tell it *"found the passport"* or *"I can't find the ladder"*, ask *"which room
should I check next?"*, or go through a room together (*"I'm in the garage: the drill's here, the saw's in the shed
now"*). It's the same review as in the app, so you can start with Claude and finish on your phone.
