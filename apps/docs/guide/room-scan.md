# Scanning a room

Photograph a room once and Ferret lists what's in it: the sofa, the TV, the bookshelf, the floor lamp — each with a
category and how many. You check the list, and the things you keep become items in that room, each with its own
picture cut from the photo. It's the quickest way to get a furnished room into Ferret.

## 1. Photograph

On a room's page, **+ Add → Scan a room** (or **Scan this room** under the add box). One wide photo is usually
enough; for a big room take up to five, from different corners. Then **Find things**.

A box works too: on a box's page, **Scan what's inside** — what's found goes in the box.

Ferret looks at the photos in the background — a few minutes a photo. You don't have to wait: the bell says
**Room scan ready to review** when it's done.

## 2. Review

The photo shows a numbered outline round each thing found, and the list below says what it is. Everything starts
ticked:

- **Untick** what you don't want to keep track of;
- fix a **name**, the **count** (four dining chairs are one line, ×4) or the **category**;
- **missed something?** Tap it on the photo, and type what it is.

**Add N things** makes them in the room. Each gets its own photo, cut from the room's, and its history says *Added …
from a room scan*; its page links back to the room photo. **Discard scan** throws the scan away and adds nothing.

Sometimes Ferret finds the things but not where they are in the photo; then the list comes without outlines, and the
things are added without their own pictures.

## Good to know

- Each photo is one AI action (see [Plan and usage](./plan-and-usage)) — however many things are in it. Plans without AI
  don't have room scans, and they need the AI features on a home Ferret (see [AI features](../hosting/ai)).
- Nothing is added until you say: what Ferret thinks it sees is only a suggestion.
- Not on the public demo — try it on your own Ferret.
- Good light and a wide angle help. Small things in drawers are better done with a
  [walkthrough](./walkthrough), one photo each.
- No signal? The offline screen has **Scan this room** once you've picked where you are: the photos wait on the phone
  and the scan starts when there's signal again.
- Claude can do it too: send it photos of a room and it lists what it found, then adds the ones you choose.
