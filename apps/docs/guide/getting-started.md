# Signing in and households

## Your account

Sign in with your email and password. The very first account on a Ferret server can sign up straight away; everyone
after that is **invited** (see below) and confirms their email address before they can sign in.

Forgot your password? **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page emails you a link — if whoever runs Ferret has
set up email. If not, ask them.

## Getting started

A new household's Home shows a **Getting started** checklist: add your home, its rooms, choose categories, add your
first things (walk through a room, or import), and invite your household. Each step ticks itself when it's done; the
checklist goes away once they all are, or when you **Hide** it.

## Getting around

![Ferret's home screen on a phone: search, what it's all worth, places, and the tab bar](/screenshots/home-light.webp){.shot .light-only}
![Ferret's home screen on a phone: search, what it's all worth, places, and the tab bar](/screenshots/home-dark.webp){.shot .dark-only}

- **On a phone**, the tabs along the bottom: **Home**, **Everything**, **Places**, **Maintenance** and **More** — More has
  everything else (lists, insurance, settings, help, appearance, sign out), grouped.
- **On a computer**, the same places are along the top, and the rest is in the menu under your initial. On a big screen
  everything is in the sidebar on the left — **Collapse** at its foot folds it to icons (hover for names) — and the
  menu under your initial is just appearance and signing out.
- **+ Add** (the round button on a phone, top right on a computer) is every way to add something: type it in, from
  photos, scan a barcode, walk through a room, import, or print blank QR labels. Looking at a room, category or tag,
  new things go there.
- **Search** and **scan** stay in the top bar as you scroll, on every screen: the search box itself on the home
  screens, and a search field and scan button elsewhere that take you straight to it. Press <kbd>/</kbd> or
  <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> to jump to it from anywhere.

## Households

Everything in Ferret belongs to a **household**: its properties, rooms, items, warranties and maintenance. Everyone in
a household sees the same inventory. You can be in more than one (say, your home and your parents'); the household's
name is shown in the top bar, and the menu (or its name in the top bar) switches between them.

Each household has a **time zone** (**Household & settings → Time zone**): what "today" means for due dates, overdue
and ending soon, and when reminders arrive. A new household starts with the time zone of the device that made it; the
owner and admins can change it.

## Inviting people

**Household & settings → Household → Invite** makes an invitation for one email address and a role. When your Ferret
can send email (it can on a hosted Ferret; at home, [set up email](../hosting/email)), Ferret **emails them the
link** to join. Otherwise it doesn't send anything — copy the link and send it yourself (Messages, email…). Either way
**Copy** and **Share** are there too, and the link works for that address only, for two weeks.

Under **Waiting to join** you can see when each invitation was emailed, **Send again** (it also gives the link another
two weeks), copy its **Link** again, or **Cancel** it. A household emails at most 20 invitations a day.

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including removing the household. |
| **Admin** | Everything but removing the household: invite people, change roles, [download everything](./download-everything). |
| **Member** | Add and change things. |
| **Viewer** | Look and search only — handy for an insurance agent or a house-sitter. |

People can leave a household themselves; owners and admins can remove them or change their role.
