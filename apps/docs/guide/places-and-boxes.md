# Places and boxes

## Properties and rooms

![A room: the places inside it, a box to add things to, and the things kept there](/screenshots/room-light.webp){.shot .light-only}
![A room: the places inside it, a box to add things to, and the things kept there](/screenshots/room-dark.webp){.shot .dark-only}

**Places** holds your **properties** — a home, a cabin, a storage unit — and inside each, rooms and the places in
them, as deep as you like: *Highland House › Garage › Shelf 2*. Add, rename or move them from **Places**; a place can
be removed once nothing is in it.

A property can have its address, the year it was built and what it's worth. With an address, **Open in Maps** shows
it, and Ferret can show a small map picture (if whoever runs Ferret has set up Apple Maps).

Open a property or a room to see what's in it, with the places inside it as tiles. A room's count includes everything
in the places inside it — *12 things (3 here)* means 3 are kept in the room itself and 9 on its shelves, in its closets
and so on. **Places** shows the same counts beside each place.

### Documents and photos of a place {#files}

Some papers belong to the house rather than to a thing in it: the deed, the insurance policy, a home inspection report,
the paint colours, photos of the wiring before the wall was closed up. Open the property or the room and tap **Add a
document or photo** (or **Add** beside **Documents & photos** once it has some). They're kept encrypted like a thing's
files and come with [Download everything](./download-everything). Removing a place or a property takes its files to
[Recently deleted](./recently-deleted) with it.

Ferret reads them for their words, so search finds them: *sump pump* finds the inspection report that mentions it,
under **In documents** below the things. A document's text is read without the AI where it can be (it doesn't count
as an AI action); a photo, or a scan with no text in it, is read by the AI. Nothing is suggested from them — they
aren't about one thing.

## Boxes

A box, bin or case is an item marked **This is a box, bin or case**. Things go *in* a box rather than in a room, and
they're wherever the box is: move the box to the attic and everything in it moves too.

Box things up from an item's page (choose its box) or from the box (add what's in it).
