# Lending and borrowing

Lent the ladder to a neighbour? Borrowed a tile saw? Ferret remembers who has what.

## Lending something

On the thing's page, **Lend or borrow…** → **Mine, lent to someone**: who has it, since when, and (optional) when it's
due back. Its page then says *With Sam since May 3*, and so does every list it's in.

**Reminders to them**: add their email and choose how often — every week, two weeks or month. Ferret sends a friendly
email that it's still with them (the first on the due date, if there is one), with a link where they can say
**I've given it back** — you'll see *Sam says it's back* in the bell, to agree with — or **Stop these reminders**.
(Email needs your Ferret to be able to send it: see [Email](../hosting/email). The link in the email works only where
Ferret can be reached — see [Away from home](../hosting/away-from-home).)

When it's back: **It's back**, on its page or under **Lent & borrowed**.

## Borrowing something

**Lend or borrow…** → **Someone's, borrowed by us**, with when it's due back. The bell (and the daily reminder)
tells you when it's time to give it back.

## Keeping track

**Lent & borrowed** in the menu lists everything that's out, overdue first. The bell mentions things lent out
past their due date, or out for two months with no due date. Each thing's [history](./finding-things#a-thing-s-history)
keeps every loan. Claude can see them too (*"who has the ladder?"*).
