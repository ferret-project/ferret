# Walking through a room

The quickest way to get a whole house into Ferret: one room at a time, photograph everything, then name it all in one go.
(For a furnished room, [scanning the room](./room-scan) — one wide photo — is quicker still.)

## 1. Photograph

On a room's page, **Walk through this room** (or **Walk through a room** in the menu, then pick one). Then
**Take a photo** — one thing per photo — and again, and again. Each photo becomes an item in that room straight away;
they upload in the background while you carry on, with a tick on each one that's in. **Or pick from your photos** adds
ones you've already taken.

## 2. Review

**Review N things** goes through them one at a time: the photo, what Ferret read off it (*"A red cordless drill with a
battery pack"*), and a name ready to confirm. Anything else it could read — brand, model, serial number — is ticked to
save too; untick what's wrong (tap the photo to see it full screen and zoom in on a label). Then:

- **Save & next**;
- **Skip** — it stays a *New item* (the bell keeps reminding you), to do later;
- **Not worth keeping** — delete it (it's in [Recently deleted](./recently-deleted) for 30 days).

If a photo is still being read, the card says so and fills itself in when it's done — or just type the name.

Reading photos needs the AI features (see [AI features](../hosting/ai)); without them you name each one yourself, which is
still quick.

## Tips

- Good light, one thing filling the frame. A second photo of the label or serial-number plate helps — add it from the
  item's page later.
- Boxes: photograph the box, mark it as a box, then use **Add from photos** on the box's page for what's inside.
