# Categories and tags

## Categories

A category says what a thing *is* — *Electronics › TV*, *Tools › Power tools*. Each item has one. The insurance report
groups by category.

**Categories & tags** in the menu lists them as a tree; new households can start from a starter set. A
category can have **extra fields** its items fill in (a TV's screen size, a bike's frame size); subcategories get
their parents' fields too.

A category's page lists what's in it, with its subcategories as tiles — each with a photo of something inside and how
many things it holds (its own subcategories included).

## Templates

A **template** is for something you have lots of the same kind of — coins, trading cards, dolls, toys, comics, stamps,
records, LEGO sets. It says what to fill in for each one (a coin's year, mint mark, metal and grade; a card's set,
number, player and grading company) and which category and tags they start with.

On **Categories & tags**, **Add starter templates** gives you one for each of those, or **+ Template** makes your own
(fields can be text, a number with a unit, a date, or a choice from a list). Then, adding a thing (**+ Add → Type it in**)
or editing one, choose its **Template**: its fields appear, alongside any its category has. What you fill in shows on the
thing's page and is found by search — *"mint mark S"*.

Changing a template changes what all its things show; removing one leaves what was filled in on each of them.

## Tags

Tags are free labels, as many as you like per item — *camping*, *Christmas*, *Grandma's china*. Each tag is also a
collection with its own page. Renaming a tag onto another merges them.
