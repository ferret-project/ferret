# Past things

When you sell something, give it away, donate it, throw it out, lose it or have it stolen, don't delete it — mark it. It leaves
your lists, totals, search, the insurance report, reminders and the shopping list, but keeps its page, photos, receipts
and history.

## Marking something gone

On its page, **Edit → No longer have it?**, then:

- **What happened** — sold it, gave it away, donated it, threw it out, lost it, it was stolen;
- **When** (today unless you say otherwise);
- **Sold for**, **Worth when given** for a donation, or for lost and stolen things **Insurance paid**, if you like;
- a **note** — who bought it, the police report number.
- what **replaced** it, if anything: both pages then link the other ([more](./repairs#replaced-by)).

A box has to be empty first. Its maintenance stops, and its warranties drop off the lists.

## Past things

**Past things** (in the menu) lists them by what happened, with what things sold for in total. Its page shows what
happened, with **Bring it back** if that was a mistake (or you bought it back).

## Lost or stolen: for a claim

If anything was lost or stolen, Past things offers **Download for a claim (CSV)**: those things with what happened and
when first, then everything else the [insurance report](./insurance-report) has — where it was, make, model, serial
number, what you paid, what it was worth, its photos and receipts.

## Donations

Choose **Donated it** for things given to a charity or organisation. Say who it went to (**Given to**), what it was
worth when you gave it, and add the receipt they gave you if you have one — it goes on the thing's page with its other
documents.

Past things then shows **Donations by year**: each year with how many things and what they were worth. Open a year for
a page to **print or save as PDF** — the date, who it went to, what it was, its condition, what you paid and what it
was worth, with a total — or **Download spreadsheet (CSV)**.

Ferret keeps a record of what you gave; it doesn't decide what's tax-deductible or what a donation is worth for tax.
That's for you or your accountant (and the organisation's own receipt is what usually counts). A year is the calendar
year of the date given.

**Gave it away** is still there for things given to a person.

Deleting is different: it's for mistakes and duplicates, and goes for good after 30 days (see
[Recently deleted](./recently-deleted)).
